Business, at its best, is a bridge — between heritage and markets, between profit and people.

I'm a Grade 12 student with a strong interest in business, economics, entrepreneurship and social impact.

Through initiatives such as Vellura Baking Co. and CraftGuard, I've explored how business can support communities while creating sustainable opportunities. My experiences in research, internships and student ventures have strengthened my interest in business strategy, finance, and building ventures that address real-world challenges.

I hope to pursue business studies and continue developing ideas that combine commercial thinking with meaningful impact.
